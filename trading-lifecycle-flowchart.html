EA Execution Loops

  • Tick Loop (OnTick): Evaluates entry signals, safety filters, gap distance, and hard close triggers.
  • Modification Loop (500ms Timer): Manages step-wise SL ladder updates, anti-churn rate limiting, partial basket locks, and 20X live price SL ratchets.
  • Broker Interaction: All actions route through SafeOrderSend(), SafeOrderModify(), and SafeOrderClose() with slippage and error 130 guards.

Risk Control Features

  • Candle Throttling: Maximum 2 orders allowed per candle (IsOneCandleOrderAllowed()) with restart persistence.
  • Profit Ratchet Floor: Rejects any SL modification that would lower already protected profit.
  • Parent-Linked Recovery: Deep gap recovery orders never close alone while parent is open; pair exits together at >= $1.00 net profit.
  • Fixed Gap 20X Ratchet (V7010): Orders advancing 20 points into profit lock live market price every 20 points.

Anti-Churn & Broker Safety Rules (V7002-V7010 Core Guards)

  • Profit Ratchet Floor: An order will NEVER be modified if the proposed new SL locks LESS profit than the current SL. For BUY orders, SL can never move downward; for SELL orders, SL can never move upward.
  • Losing Order Guard (V7009): Losing orders are strictly protected from erroneous modification to avoid widening risk.
  • Minimum $10 SL Improvement: If an order already has an active SL, it will not be modified unless the new SL improves by at least $10.0 raw BTC (or $2.0 in FixedGAP mode) to prevent server lag.
  • Minimum $15 Live Price Gap: The proposed SL must stay at least $15 (or broker stop level) away from the live Bid/Ask to avoid broker rejection (Error 130).

Opening Engine: When Recovery Orders Open

  • Big Adverse Gap Requirement: The live price must have pulled back at least RecoveryMinDistanceRaw = 2000 raw BTC points away from the parent order's open price.
  • Parent Loss Threshold: The parent trade's floating loss must reach dynamicRecoveryLossLimit <= -$1.00.
  • Direction Resumption Confirmation:
    • For BUY Parent: GlobalSSLDirection == 1 || EMADirection == 1
    • For SELL Parent: GlobalSSLDirection == -1 || EMADirection == -1
  • Lot Size Multiplier & Hard Cap: Recovery order opens with parentLots * RecoveryLotMultiplier (2.0x volume), strictly capped at RecoveryMaxLots = 0.05 (even if 2X parent volume exceeds 0.05, lot is clamped to 0.05) with comment RECOVERY_<ParentTicket>.
  • Order Throttling: Strictly respects MaxRecoveryOrders = 5 and maximum 2 orders per candle.

Exit Engine: Two Clear Exit Rules

  • Rule 1: Active Parent Pair (Combined Basket Exit >= $1.00):
    As long as the parent order is open and exists, the recovery order will NEVER close alone. The recovery order stays bound to the parent trade until their combined net profit (RecoveryProfit + ParentProfit) reaches >= $1.00 (RecoveryBasketProfitUSD). When reached, BOTH trades close together, completely eliminating the parent's loss and banking profit!
  • Rule 2: Clean Orphan Exit (>= $1.00 with Parent Closed):
    If the parent was closed previously (via Stop Loss, manual close, or basket exit), the recovery order becomes an orphan. Once recoveryProfit >= $1.00, the EA closes the orphan cleanly at market without attempting to close the non-existent parent order.
  • Dashboard Display: Live order list displays parent floating P/L alongside recovery profit: +5.19(-$10.09) for transparent monitoring.

V7012 5-Stage Profit Extraction Architecture

An integrated suite of 5 algorithmic profit multipliers designed to extract maximum gains from trending BTCUSD runs while locking realized cash directly into account balance.

1

Tiered Breathing Room Anti-Whipsaw

Eliminates premature Stop Loss triggers on Rung 1. Instead of suffocating the order at live price, Rung 1 locks a comfortable +$5.0 raw BTC cushion above entry, leaving 15 points of breathing space to reach higher rungs.

Rung 1: OpenPrice ± $5.0 | Rung X: Locks Rung (X-1)
2

Partial Profit Taking Scale-Out

When floating net profit reaches ≥ $1.00, the EA immediately closes 40% of the position (0.02 of 0.05 lot) at market. Realized cash is deposited directly into balance, leaving a 0.03 lot runner to ride multi-hundred point expansions.

Close 40% @ $1.00 → 60% Runner Trails
3

Dynamic Recovery Profit Trail Runaway Bounce

When combined basket profit reaches ≥ $1.00, the EA closes the losing parent trade first to eliminate all drawdown. The 2X recovery order's Stop Loss is then ratcheted directly into profit, trailing the bounce for an extra +50 to +200 points!

Kill Drawdown Parent → Let 2X Recovery Trail
4

ATR Volatility-Adaptive Step Adaptive Rungs

Continuously computes M5 Average True Range (ATR). During violent Bitcoin volatility spikes, the ladder step automatically expands from 20.0 to MathMax(20.0, ATR * 0.5), preventing noise shakes while maintaining rapid 20-gap locks during calm sessions.

Trigger Step = MathMax(20.0, M5 ATR × 0.5)
5

Risk-Free Trend Pyramiding Zero Capital Risk

When an existing winner has secured ≥ $2.00 profit with its Stop Loss strictly in profit above entry, the EA opens an add-on position (0.01–0.02 lots) in the direction of the dominant SSL trend. The add-on is 100% funded by the locked profit of the base trade—zero risk to base account equity!

Locked Profit ≥ $2.00 → Open PYRAMID Add-on (Max 2 Active)
