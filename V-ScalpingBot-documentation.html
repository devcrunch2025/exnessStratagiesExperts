Institutional Low-Latency & Server-Call Optimization Matrix

Optimized for ultra-fast BTCUSD execution on Exness terminals. Reduces tick-to-trade latency, eliminates redundant indicator recalculations, and prevents broker network lockups:

1. Momentum Loop Throttle

Replaced the unthrottled 500-bar historical scan on every tick with a 1-bar closed tick check and bar-change refresh, eliminating up to 3,000 redundant iADX indicator calls per price tick.

2. SSL Tick-Sequence Caching

CalculateSSL(0) and CalculateSSL(1) are now computed once per tick sequence and cached. Reused instantly across signal checks, eliminating ~800 redundant iMA calculations per tick.

3. Order Count Snapshot Caching

GetTotalBuyOrders(), GetTotalSellOrders(), and GetTotalEAOrders() utilize tick-sequence caching. Cuts 20+ redundant OrdersTotal() loops into $O(1)$ instant lookups.

4. Loop-Level MarketInfo Hoisting

Moved MODE_TICKVALUE, MODE_TICKSIZE, MODE_STOPLEVEL, and RefreshRates() outside order modification loops, preventing mid-loop rate desyncs and CPU churn.

5. SafeOrderClose Routing & Slippage Protection

Replaced unthrottled raw OrderClose() with SafeOrderClose() using broker-compliant slippage, preventing ERR_REQUOTE (error 138) and ERR_PRICE_CHANGED (error 135) during fast BTCUSD volatility.

The "Never Close Immediately" Modify Architecture

Instead of closing positions via OrderClose() and eating market spread penalties, the robot transforms close signals into high-precision Stop Loss modifications. It tightens risk on losers and ratchets profit on winners.

When Order Is in Profit (P/L > 0)

The engine computes the nearest broker-compliant distance:
minSafeDistance = MathMax(stopLevel, (Ask - Bid) * 2.0).

• BUY Position: SL is pulled to Bid - minSafeDistance (guaranteeing locked profit above open price).
• SELL Position: SL is pulled to Ask + minSafeDistance (guaranteeing locked profit below open price).

Result: Order is safely protected without leaving money on the table; if price continues in favor, the trade remains open to run!

When Order Is in Loss (P/L < 0)

Rather than dumping the order at an unfavorable bid price, the engine sets a fixed $50 raw gap buffer:

• BUY Position: newSL = openPrice - 50.0
• SELL Position: newSL = openPrice + 50.0

Result: Caps further catastrophic drawdown while giving the Bitcoin price breathing room to mean-revert or trigger a paired recovery order.

Unblocked Recovery Modification: Previously, SafeOrderModify() rejected any ticket beginning with RECOVERY_. With the updated architecture, recovery orders can now participate in full Stop Loss ratcheting, locking in profits incrementally instead of forcing immediate terminations.
